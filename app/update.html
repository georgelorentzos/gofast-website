Update the app

Stay updated for the best experience and get all the latest features and fixes.

How to update

1

Open your store

Tap the badge above — it takes you straight to GoFast on Google Play or the App Store.

2

Tap Update

If a new version is available you will see an Update button. Tap it and wait a few seconds.

3

Open GoFast

That's it — you are on the latest version. The live map opens instantly, ready for your next ride.

Why keep GoFast updated

Every update makes finding your taxi faster and more reliable — smoother live tracking, clearer routes, and better calls between you and the driver.

Updates also fix issues reported by riders and drivers on Thassos, so the newest version is always the best one.
